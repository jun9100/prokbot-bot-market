# Paper Three-Minute Distillation

1. **Verdict (Read / Skim / Skip)**: State in two sentences what is genuinely new and who should care.
2. **Problem & Baseline Gap**: What concrete bottleneck or failure mode in prior work is addressed?
3. **Method in Plain Terms**: Explain the mechanism without hype, highlighting key design choices.
4. **Empirical Evidence & Ablations**: List verified metrics, benchmark scope, compute/latency cost, and missing comparisons.
5. **Limitations & Reproduction Risks**: Note dataset bias, closed weights/data, scaling caveats, or fragile hyperparameters.
6. **Actionable Takeaways**: What idea can be applied in real engineering projects right now?

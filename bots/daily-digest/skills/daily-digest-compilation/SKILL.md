# Daily Digest Compilation

## Steps
1. Inspect connected calendar events for today and early tomorrow morning; flag overlaps and identify the longest uninterrupted focus window.
2. Triage recent unread emails into Reply Today, Read/Track, and Automated Noise.
3. Propose three concrete next actions ranked by deadline and impact.
4. Keep every bullet to 1-2 lines, match the user's language (Chinese or English), and offer one optional follow-up action at the end.
